Dictionary Form (辞書形 - Jishokei): 
	always present tense, affirmative (not negative), and casual/plain.
	All Japanese verbs in the dictionary form end in an "u" sound (like _taberu_, _iku_, _suru_).
	Think of it as the "Base Form" of a verb.

Plain Form (普通形 - Futsuukei)
	the entire family of casual/informal Japanese words. It includes the dictionary form, but it also covers past tense, negative forms, and past-negative forms.
	Basically, "Plain Form" means "not polite" (no _~masu_ for verbs, no _~desu_ for nouns/adjectives).

- んです is easy version of のです
- ~ば form is simply 〜eば 
- Passive is simply ~aれば
- Prohibitive is simple 〜な
- そうです vs ようです: outward appearance vs outward appearance and read/heard/told
- Imperative of くれる (group 2, ru verb) is くれ not くれろ
- てみます try sth
- たばかりjust
- たら
- たまま
- させてくれる
- させていただく
- てもらいます　(passive structure but no passive)
- によると (according to)
- によって（"by" in passive creative v）
- Receiver は Doer に Object を Passive
- Senior は Junior を Causative
- Senior は Junior に Object を Causative
- Causative prefer を、passive always に

- 分かる、できる have no potential form

V1(stem)ながら、V2
	V2 is always the main action. V1 is just the background action happening at the same time. 音楽を聞きながら勉強します (I study while listening to music)
Vていません
	This is used to express that an action has not yet taken place up until this moment. If someone asks you, "Have you eaten lunch yet?", you cannot answer with the simple past negative «Tabemasen deshita» (I did not eat). That implies you didn't eat in the past and the window has closed. To say you haven't done it yet but intend to, you must use ⁠Vていません⁠, usually accompanied by the word まだ (mada - yet).

Grouping Rules
	most verbs are group 1 (u)
	all aru, oru, uru are group 1
	most iru, eru group 2 (ru)
	so consider everything group 1 until you see iru, eru. then think a lil bit!
	→bitch verbs: 
		要る【い・る】(u-verb) - to need
		帰る【かえ・る】(u-verb) - to go home
		切る【き・る】(u-verb) - to cut
		しゃべる (u-verb) - to talk
		知る【し・る】(u-verb) - to know
		⼊る【はい・る】(u-verb) - to enter
		⾛る【はし・る】(u-verb) - to run
Imperative Spectrum
	命令形 (食べろ)
	～なさい （起きなさい）
	～て（待って）
	～てください（食べてください）
	～てもらえませんか
	～ていただけませんか（食べていただけませんか）
〜てしまう→ちゃう
	死んでしまった -> 死んじゃった
	忘れてしまった -> 忘れちゃった
